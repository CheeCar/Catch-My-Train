    How It Works

    Menu bar

    Click the train in the menu bar at the top of your screen. The panel stays there after you close the window.

    Widgets

    Add them from the widget gallery on your desktop. They show the same station as the window, and their buttons change the station, refresh or go to standby.

    All times on this page are made up. They don't come from any transit operator.

    Station names, line names, route symbols and line colors come from each operator's published data and belong to that operator. They appear here only to identify the service.
